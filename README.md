# Crisis Radar

Crisis-only cut of the supervisory radar. Same single-file app: front page, stress and resolution agenda, themes, desk, calendar, map, and Monday's brief. Supervisory and regulatory publications are removed.

Kept: geopolitical reverse stress test, ESRB frontier-AI warning and the 31 October action-plan request, resolution / CMDI continuum, DGS available-financial-means consultation, and the Fed 2026 stress-test results.

Rebuild: `./scripts/build.sh`

Open `index.html` in a browser. Map, fonts, and PDF export need the internet.
