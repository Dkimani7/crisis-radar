Executive summary

One disclosed bank cash loss. Fideuram, the private bank of Intesa Sanpaolo, sent €95 million after a WhatsApp that looked like the chief executive and a call that used an AI clone of a lawyer’s voice. About €53 million was recovered. About €36 million is still missing. Korean banks lost customer records, not funds. Mythos is a capability shift, not a booked loss.

  • Deepfake fraud, Fideuram. February, reported 25 September. Transfers went mainly to China and Hong Kong, then into crypto. The then-chairman resigned. The bank declined to comment. Reuters ↗
  • AI intrusion, South Korea. 28 September to 6 October. Shinhan, KB Kookmin, Hana, BNK Busan, two savings banks and Hyundai Capital. About 66,000 people. Entry was a loan-broker portal. Investigators point to Artex. No funds stolen. Reuters ↗
  • Model capability, Mythos. Anthropic’s Claude Mythos, April, limited release. Nikkei on 2 October: Japanese banks finding about twice the flaws. Palo Alto found 75 in a month. Nikkei Asia ↗
Supervisory angle. The ESAs’ autumn update groups capable AI models with cyber risk, and names dependence on non-EU ICT providers as the amplifier. Source: ESMA, 9 Sep 2026.
